### Design

**Question:** What trends exist in SUID cases involving infants who have been exposed to domestic violence or abuse between 2008 and 2019?

**Design Type:** Trends within cases. Compare proportions among the cases across periods.

When a case series' coverage changes over time, changes in case counts are not changes in incidence.

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series**: ncfrp_v51
  - Covers: child deaths reviewed by participating fatality review teams; deaths only, no births or surviving infants.
  - Period: 2008-2019 (the manuscript uses this period).
  - Missing answers: documented per item; unknown is a separate answer.

- **Population Reference**: prams_p8_arf
  - Covers: weighted survey of mothers a few months after live births, sampled from birth certificates in participating states.
  - Period: Phase 8 (2016-2021).
  - Missing answers: documented per item; unknown is a separate answer.

**Note:** The period for prams_p8_arf does not fully overlap with ncfrp_v51, but we can still compare trends within the available overlapping years between 2008 and 2019.

### Period

- **Case Series**: 2008-2019
- **Population Reference**: 2016-2021 (using only data from 2016 to 2019 for comparison)

### Variables

**Domestic Violence or Abuse:**

- **Case Series (ncfrp_v51)**
  - Variable: INFipvvic, "Child have history of intimate partner violence - Yes, as victim"
    - Values: 1 = yes; 2 = no; 9 = unknown
  - Variable: GIV1malperpneg, "Caregiver has history of child maltreatment - Perpetrator - Yes, Neglect"
    - Values: 1 = yes; 0 = no; 9 = unknown

- **Population Reference (prams_p8_arf)**
  - Variable: PAD6HUS, "Abuse --during preg, h/p"
    - Values: .T (teen mom not asked), 1 = no; 2 = yes
  - Variable: PAB_XHUS, "Abuse -- 12 mnths b4 preg, ex-h/p"
    - Values: .T (teen mom not asked), 1 = no; 2 = yes

**Comparison:** The variables measure the same concept but differ in wording and timing. Case series data is specific to child abuse history, while population reference surveys maternal exposure during pregnancy.

### Biases

- **Selection Bias:** 
  - ncfrp_v51 captures cases reported by fatality review teams; participation varies over time.
  - prams_p8_arf samples mothers of live births from birth certificates, not designed to count infant deaths.

- **Information Bias:**
  - ncfrp_v51 collects detailed information on child maltreatment and abuse during reviews.
  - prams_p8_arf surveys maternal exposure during pregnancy, not necessarily the same timeframe or detail as case series data.

- **Confounding:**
  - Potential confounders include socioeconomic status, prenatal care quality, and environmental factors that are not uniformly measured across sources.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

- Incidence rates of SUID cases involving domestic violence or abuse cannot be estimated due to lack of denominator.
- Causal relationships cannot be established; only trends in proportions among cases can be observed.

### Prior Art

The closest claim is:

**suid_literature_abstracts.C10**: Maternal smoking during pregnancy significantly increases the risk of SUID. This study focuses on a different exposure (domestic violence or abuse) and does not address trends over time specifically for this population.

### Verdict

These data can be used to examine trends in proportions of SUID cases involving domestic violence or abuse within the case series from 2008-2019. However, they cannot establish incidence rates or causal relationships due to lack of a denominator source and differences in measurement timing between sources. Additionally, the period for prams_p8_arf only partially overlaps with ncfrp_v51, limiting direct comparisons.

**Data Element Missing:** A denominator source covering 2008-2019 would be needed to estimate incidence rates or compare proportions relative to live births in that period.
