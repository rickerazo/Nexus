### Design

**Design:** Trends within cases. Compare proportions among the SUID cases across periods.

**Restated Question:** Is there a temporal pattern in the proportion of Sudden Unexpected Infant Death (SUID) cases involving infants with sleep environment issues and mothers reporting substance use during pregnancy from 2008 to 2019?

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series: ncfrp_v51**
  - Covers child deaths reviewed by participating fatality review teams, focusing on SUID cases.
  - Provides information on infant sleep environment issues and maternal substance use.

- **Population Reference: prams_p7_arf**
  - Weighted survey of mothers a few months after live births in states that participated from 2012 to 2015.
  - Provides data on maternal smoking during pregnancy, which serves as the proxy for general substance use.

### Period

- **Period:** The study requires trends from 2008 to 2019. 
  - `ncfrp_v51` covers SUID cases from 2004 to 2019.
  - Additional years (2016-2019) would be needed for consistency with the proposed period.
  - `prams_p7_arf` covers 2012-2015, which is insufficient; additional data from 2016 to 2019 must be acquired.

### Variables

**Sleep Environment Issues (ncfrp_v51)**
- **INF3nicudrug:** Infant born drug exposed.
- **INFexpipv:** During pregnancy did mother experience intimate partner violence.
- **INFuseddrug:** During pregnancy did mother use illicit drugs.
- **INFusedalc:** During pregnancy did mother have heavy alcohol use.
- **CIRdeathsleepenv:** Was the death related to sleeping or the sleep environment?
- **INF5smoke:** Infant in room with someone smoking.

**Maternal Substance Use (ncfrp_v51 and prams_p7_arf)**
- **INF3nicudrug, INFexpipv, INFuseddrug, INFusedalc:** These variables capture specific substance use issues.
- **MOMSMOKE (prams_p8_arf):** Did Mom smoke? Note: This variable is coded “yes” if Mom smoked in any of the three trimesters.

### Biases

1. **Selection Bias**
   - `ncfrp_v51` cases are reviewed by fatality review teams, which may lead to selection bias.
   - Participating states vary over time; changes in participation could introduce biases not reflective of true incidence trends.
  
2. **Information Bias**
   - Data on sleep environment and substance use might be underreported or inaccurately reported due to the nature of case reviews.
   - Maternal smoking data from `prams_p7_arf` may miss other types of substance abuse.

3. **Confounding Variables**
   - Socioeconomic status, urban/rural differences, healthcare access, and prenatal care quality could confound results but are not measured in these datasets.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

- The design can only establish trends within cases (proportions) and does not estimate incidence or risk.
- It cannot account for changes in case ascertainment over time unless data on participation patterns are provided.

### Prior Art

None of the claims from existing manuscripts directly address temporal trends in sleep environment issues combined with maternal substance use. The closest claim is `bedsharing_ms_v7.C8`, which notes an association between bed sharing and smoking, but it does not examine these factors over time or together comprehensively.

### Verdict

**Can Answer:** Yes, the data can be used to explore trends in sleep environment issues and maternal substance use among SUID cases from 2008 to 2019. However, acquiring additional PRAMS Phase 8 (2016-2019) data is necessary for a comprehensive temporal analysis.

**Limitations:**
- The study will be limited by the selection biases inherent in case reviews and the lack of detailed information on confounding factors.
- Trends must be interpreted carefully given potential changes in reporting practices and participation over time.
