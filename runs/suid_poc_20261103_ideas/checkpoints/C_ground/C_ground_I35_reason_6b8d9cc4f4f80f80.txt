### Design

**Question Restated:** How does maternal mental health history correlate specifically with sudden unexpected infant death (SUID) compared to other causes of infant death?

The design is a case-case comparison. We will compare the cases of SUID with those of other causes of infant death recorded in the same case series, focusing on maternal mental health history as a key variable.

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series:** ncfrp_v51
  - Reason: This dataset records specific details about child deaths reviewed by fatality review teams and includes SUID cases. It does not provide incidence denominators or birth data.
  
- **Population Reference:** prams_p8_arf, prams_p7_arf
  - Reason: These surveys collect detailed information on the health history of mothers after live births, including maternal mental health conditions.

### Period

**Years Needed:**
- Case Series (ncfrp_v51): 2016-2021 (manuscript uses this period)
- Population Reference (prams_p8_arf): 2016-2021
- Population Reference (prams_p7_arf): 2012-2015

### Variables

**Maternal Mental Health History:**
- **Case Series (ncfrp_v51)**
  - `INFmedical`: During pregnancy did mother have medical complications/infections 
  - `INF4referinfmh`: If yes, family referred - Infant mental health program
  - `INFprevmedical`: Did mother have medical complications in previous pregnancies

- **Population Reference (prams_p8_arf)**
  - `PG8_ANX`: Health prob during preg -- Anxiety
  - `PG8_NAUS`: Health prob during preg -- Nausea/vomiting/dehydration 
  - `PREV_DEPR`: Pre-pregnancy check for depression/anxiety

- **Population Reference (prams_p7_arf)**
  - `PREV_DEPR`: Pre-pregnancy check for depression/anxiety
  - `PG8_ANX`: Health prob during preg -- Anxiety

### Biases

**Selection Bias:**
- The case series (ncfrp_v51) covers only child deaths reviewed by fatality review teams, not all infant deaths. This may introduce selection bias.
  
**Information Bias:**
- Different sources measure maternal mental health history at different times and from different perspectives:
  - Case Series measures conditions up to the time of death.
  - Population Reference surveys mothers a few months after birth about their mental health during pregnancy.

**Confounding:**
- Social determinants (e.g., socioeconomic status) may confound the relationship between maternal mental health history and SUID/other causes of infant death. However, neither source directly measures these variables.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

The design cannot establish causation; it can only identify associations due to its observational nature. Additionally, without a denominator source, the study cannot estimate incidence rates or risk ratios, only proportions among cases.
