### Design

**Question**: How does the recording of medical complications during pregnancy differ between NCFRP_v51 case series and PRAMS_P7_ARF population reference?

This is a measurement comparison design, which aims to compare how different sources measure the same concept. Specifically, it compares detailed medical complication records in the NCFRP_v51 case series with broader categories of medical complications from the PRAMS_P7_ARF population reference.

### Sources by Role
- **Case Series**: ncfrp_v51
  - Covers: Detailed records of child deaths reviewed by fatality review teams.
- **Population Reference**: prams_p7_arf
  - Covers: Weighted survey data from mothers a few months after live births, not designed to count infant deaths.

### Period
**Period Range**: 2012-2015

This range covers the period of PRAMS_P7_ARF (Phase 7) and overlaps with the time frame used for NCFRP_v51 data provided in the manuscript context (although exact years are not specified, it is implied to be relevant).

### Variables
#### Medical Complications during Pregnancy:
- **NCFRP_v51**: Detailed records of medical complications such as pre-eclampsia (INF4preeclampsiaref), gestational hypertension (INF4preghyperref), preterm labor (INF4pretermlabref), eclampsia (INF4eclampsiarf), diabetes, gestational (INF4diabgestref), chronic lung disease (INFchronlung), seizure disorder (INF4seizureref), bacterial vaginosis (INF4bvref), Group B strep (INF4strepref), sickle cell disease (INF4sickleref).
  - **Coding**: Detailed medical conditions, with specific referral and timeframe variables.
- **PRAMS_P7_ARF**:
  - Pre-pregnancy hypertension: RF_PHYPE
  - Gestational diabetes: PG_GDB8
  - Previous preterm births: P_PRTERM
  - Hypertension eclampsia: RF_EHYPE (not available before 2020)
  - Prepregnancy diabetes: RF_PDIAB (not available before 2020)

#### Missing Answers and Coding:
- **NCFRP_v51**: Specific documentation of missing answers, unknowns.
- **PRAMS_P7_ARF**:
  - Missing answers are coded as "B" for don't know or blank, "S" for skipped, and "U" for unknown.

### Biases
#### Selection Bias
- NCFRP_v51: Only includes cases reviewed by fatality review teams, which may not be representative of all child deaths.
- PRAMS_P7_ARF: Surveys mothers post-live birth; does not capture information on infant deaths and is biased towards live births.

#### Information Bias
- **Timing**: NCFRP_v51 records complications as part of a detailed review process after the death, while PRAMS_P7_ARF collects information from surveys conducted a few months postpartum.
- **Reporting**: Medical details in NCFRP_v51 are likely more thorough and detailed than self-reported data by mothers.

#### Confounding
- Differences in how complications are recorded (e.g., pre-pregnancy vs. gestational hypertension) may confound comparisons between the two sources.
  
### What the Design Cannot Establish
The design can compare how different variables are recorded but cannot establish causality or incidence rates due to lack of a denominator source.

### Prior Art
- **SUID_manuscript_v9_clean.C10**: The study acknowledges limitations in using vital record data, which may have exposure and cause-of-death misclassification issues.
- **bedsharing_ms_v7.C18**: Sleep-environment classification in case-report systems is noted to be subject to heterogeneity in ascertainment and reporting.

The closest relevant claims address methodological concerns with the sources used but do not directly compare how medical complications are recorded between different datasets.

### Verdict
**Yes**, these data can answer a version of the candidate research idea. The study will compare how detailed medical complication records differ between NCFRP_v51 (case series) and PRAMS_P7_ARF (population reference). However, it cannot establish incidence rates or causal relationships due to the absence of a denominator source.

**Data Element Needed for Incidence Rate Estimation**: A common denominator source that includes both live births and infant deaths during 2012-2015.
