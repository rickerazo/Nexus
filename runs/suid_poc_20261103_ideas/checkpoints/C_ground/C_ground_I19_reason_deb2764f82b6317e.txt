### Design

**Revised Question:** Estimate the prevalence of maternal health checks before pregnancy among mothers whose infants experienced SUID compared to those who did not, using data from ncfrp_v51 (case series), prams_p7_arf (population reference), and cdc_linkco15 (denominator).

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series:** ncfrp_v51
  - Covers: Child deaths reviewed by participating fatality review teams, focusing on SUID cases.
  - Period: 2008-2019.

- **Population Reference:** prams_p7_arf
  - Covers: Weighted survey of mothers a few months after live births in states with PRAMS participation (Phase 7).
  - Period: 2012-2015.

- **Denominator:** cdc_linkco15
  - Covers: US live births linked to infant deaths within the first year.
  - Period: 2015.

### Period

**Common period:** 2015 (the intersection of the denominator and population reference periods).

### Variables

#### Maternal Health Checks Before Pregnancy:

- **Case Series (ncfrp_v51):**
  - Variable: D341 ISSprechronicdiseasedur (Chronic disease control Education - during pregnancy)
  - Note: This variable is not ideal as it pertains to chronic disease control during pregnancy, rather than before.

- **Population Reference (prams_p7_arf):**
  - Variables:
    - PRE_CKBP (Pre-pregnancy check for high blood pressure)
    - PRE_CKD (Pre-pregnancy check for diabetes)
    - PRE_DDS (Pre-pregnancy teeth cleaned)
    - PRE_HIST (Pre-pregnancy talk fam med hist)
    - PRE_RX (Pre-pregnancy prescrip meds)
    - PRE_EXER (Pre-pregnancy exercise 3+ days/wk)
    - PRE_DIET (Pre-pregnancy dieting)

- **Denominator (cdc_linkco15):**
  - No direct variable for pre-pregnancy health checks.

### Biases

#### Selection Bias:
- The case series may not be representative of all SUID cases due to limited geographic coverage and varying levels of participation over time.
- PRAMS samples may not represent the general population of new mothers, especially given its state-specific nature.

#### Information Bias:
- ncfrp_v51 data may underreport or misclassify pre-pregnancy health checks since it focuses on death reviews rather than comprehensive maternal histories.
- Variables in prams_p7_arf might be influenced by recall bias and social desirability bias, leading to inaccuracies in self-reported behaviors.

#### Confounding:
- PRAMS captures a wide range of demographic and socioeconomic factors that could confound the relationship between pre-pregnancy health checks and SUID incidence. However, these data are not available in ncfrp_v51.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

This design can compare proportions but cannot establish causal relationships or estimate rates due to missing denominator-specific variables for pre-pregnancy health checks. It also lacks longitudinal data on maternal behaviors before pregnancy from the case series source (ncfrp_v51).

### Prior Art

- **SUID Manuscript:** Claims such as C8 and C9 discuss risk factors like smoking, but do not address pre-pregnancy health checks.
- **Bedsharing Manuscript:** Similarly focuses on post-conceptional age and prenatal/postnatal exposures but does not cover the specific topic of pre-pregnancy maternal health.

### Verdict

**Cannot answer the exact question as posed:**

The proposed design cannot establish a direct comparison between mothers with infants experiencing SUID and those who did not, due to missing variables in ncfrp_v51 that capture pre-pregnancy health checks. Additionally, prams_p7_arf does not fully cover 2015, limiting the common period for analysis.

**Missing Data Element:**
- A variable measuring pre-pregnancy health checks directly in the case series (ncfrp_v51) is required.
- Expanding PRAMS coverage to include more recent data and a broader geographic range could also improve comparability.

Alternatively, expanding the scope of ncfrp_v51 to capture more comprehensive maternal histories before pregnancy would be necessary for this study.
