### Design

**Design:** Measurement comparison. Compare how the same concept is recorded in two or more sources (wording, timing, who reports it, coding of missing answers) and ask how the difference would bias a comparison between them.

**Restated Question:**
Is there a difference in previous live births between mothers of SUID cases and those of live births? This design will compare proportions rather than incidence rates due to the lack of a denominator source for SUID cases from ncfrp_v51 (case series).

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series:** ncfrp_v51
  - **Reason:** Records detailed information on SUID cases, including prior births.
  
- **Population Reference:** prams_p8_arf, prams_p7_arf 
  - **Reason:** Provides a population-based survey of live birth mothers, capturing their pregnancy histories.

### Period

**Period:**
The overlapping period between ncfrp_v51 (2004-2019) and both PRAMS phases is essential. However, the manuscript uses data from 2008 to 2019 for SUID cases and Phase 8 (2016-2021) for live birth mothers.

### Variables

**Previous Live Births:**

- **ncfrp_v51:** 
  - `INF3livebirth`: Number of live births.
  - `INF3living`: Number living.
  
- **prams_p8_arf, prams_p7_arf:** 
  - `PRE_LB_NAPHSIS`: Number of previous live births grouped.

**Missing Answers:**
- ncfrp_v51 uses "9" or "unknown."
- PRAMS phases use ".U", ".B", and ".S".

### Biases

#### Selection Bias:
- **Case Series:** ncfrp_v51 includes only reviewed child deaths, not a representative sample of all SUID cases.
- **Population Reference:** PRAMS surveys mothers from live births but may not be fully generalizable to the broader population due to survey design and response rates.

#### Information Bias:
- **ncfrp_v51:** Reports on prior births after investigation into child death.
- **PRAMS:** Surveys mothers shortly after live birth, capturing immediate recollections.

#### Confounding:
- Differences in reporting time and context (e.g., emotional state post-loss) may influence the consistency of responses between sources.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

The design can only establish a difference in proportions or odds between groups but cannot estimate incidence rates due to missing denominator data for SUID cases from ncfrp_v51. Additionally, it cannot ascertain causation due to its observational nature.

### Prior Art

- **Manuscript Claims:**
  - **SUID Manuscripts:** Investigate factors associated with SUID incidence and patterns but do not address previous birth history.
  - **Bedsharing Manuscripts:** Focus on associations between risk factors like bed sharing, maternal smoking, and prematurity across developmental windows. These manuscripts also do not cover previous live births.

### Verdict

**Verdict:**
These data can partially answer the research question by comparing proportions of mothers with prior live births between SUID cases (ncfrp_v51) and live birth groups (PRAMS). However, due to the lack of a denominator for SUID cases in ncfrp_v51, it cannot estimate incidence rates or establish causation. The study is limited by selection bias and information biases from different reporting contexts.

### Missing Data Element
- A denominator source for SUID cases would allow for estimating incidence rates and conducting more robust comparisons.
  
Thus, the design can measure differences in previous live births but lacks sufficient data to draw broader conclusions about population-level risks.
