### Design

**Question:** Does the proportion of SUID cases involving maternal stressors change over time?

**Design Type:** Trends within cases. Compare proportions among the cases across periods. When a case series' coverage changes over time, changes in case counts are not changes in incidence.

### Sources by Role

- **Case Series (ncfrp_v51):**
  - This source provides information on child deaths reviewed by fatality review teams.
  - It covers SUID cases from 2004 to 2019 and is used to identify the presence of maternal stressors among these cases.

- **Population Reference (prams_p8_arf):**
  - PRAMS Phase 8 provides data on live births, including mothers' health and behavioral factors during pregnancy.
  - It serves as a reference for understanding general exposure patterns in the population at risk, such as healthcare visits and support services.

### Period

- **Period:** 2016-2021 (PRAMS Phase 8) 
- **Case Series Period:** 2004-2019 (ncfrp_v51)

The common period for analysis is from 2016 to 2019, as this overlaps the coverage of both sources.

### Variables

**Maternal Stressors:**

- **ncfrp_v51:** 
  - `INF4stress`: Mother experienced stressor during pregnancy
  - `CIR2socpregnancy`: Life stressors - Social/economic - Pregnancy
  - `INFexpipv`: During pregnancy did mother experience intimate partner violence
  - `INFissupport`: If yes, prenatal issues - Lack of family/social support
  - `INF3prevmedical`: Did mother have medical complications in previous pregnancies

- **prams_p8_arf:**
  - `PG8_ANX`: Health prob during preg -- Anxiety
  - `MH_PGDX8`: Health prob during preg -- Depression
  - `VAR: POST-PARTUM DEPRESSION`
  - `MM_NOMD`: BC: No medical risk factors
  - `RF_PHYPE`: Prepregnancy hypertension (2020 onwards)
  
**Healthcare Visits and Support Services:**

- **ncfrp_v51:** 
  - `INFisscare`: Access or compliance issues related to prenatal care

- **prams_p8_arf:**
  - `TYP_MH`: Health care type -- visit for depression or anxiety
  - `TYP_BC`: Health care type -- visit for family planning / birth control
  - `TYP_ILLN`: Health care type -- visit for illness
  - `TYP_OBGN`: Health care type -- checkup with OB/GYN

### Biases

**Selection Bias:**
- **Case Series (ncfrp_v51):** 
  - Case ascertainment varies by state and year as participation in the fatality review teams increases over time.
  
- **Population Reference (prams_p8_arf):**
  - Sampling biases may exist due to selective surveying of live births, potentially missing high-risk pregnancies.

**Information Bias:**
- Maternal stressors and healthcare visits are self-reported differently between case series and population reference. 
- Case reports might include more detailed information about maternal stressors because they involve in-depth reviews by fatality review teams.
  
**Confounding:**
- Socioeconomic status, urban/rural location, and other contextual factors not measured directly may influence the results.

### What the Design Cannot Establish

The design can only compare proportions of SUID cases involving maternal stressors over time. It cannot establish incidence or causation due to the absence of a denominator source for rates. The comparison is limited to case series data alone.

### Prior Art

- **Manuscript Claims:**
  - `C12`: Maternal smoking was associated with higher SUID incidence across both windows.
  - `C14`: Future research will focus on understanding the long-term consequences of separation from the mother in human infants and how it may predispose males to increased vulnerability to SIDS.

The closest claim is `C14`, which suggests that future work should explore environmental stressors, but no study has analyzed changes over time specifically regarding maternal stressors. Thus, the question remains open.

### Verdict

**Yes**, these data can answer a version of the candidate research idea: "Does the proportion of SUID cases involving maternal stressors change over time?" 

However, the design is limited to trends within cases and cannot estimate incidence or causation due to the lack of a denominator source. The study will compare proportions of SUID cases involving maternal stressors from 2016-2019 using ncfrp_v51 (case series) and prams_p8_arf (population reference).
