# Rating rubric (for blinded raters)

Rate each question or strategy on five dimensions, 1–5. Use the anchors; half points are not allowed. Add a one-line comment per dimension when you give 1 or 5.

**Feasibility** — Can this be implemented with the documented variables as specified?
1 outcome or key exposure not identifiable · 3 needs a defensible proxy · 5 implementable as written.

**Coherence** — Is it consistent with established findings and biologically plausible?
1 contradicts established findings or is implausible · 3 consistent, loosely argued · 5 consistent and well argued.

**Novelty** — Is the question open?
1 already answered · 3 incremental · 5 untested and informative.

**Importance** — If answered, would it change prevention or understanding?
1 trivial · 3 useful · 5 would change prevention or understanding.

**Interpretability** — Would a domain expert understand what result would support or refute it?
1 unclear what result would support or refute it · 3 clear with effort · 5 fully explicit.

**Novelty search.** Before scoring novelty, search PubMed for at most 10 minutes per question. Log every query in `pubmed_queries`, separated by `;`.

Also record: *recovery* (0/1/2) when asked, in a separate sheet (protocol metric 3, secondary) — does this item correspond to what the held-out manuscript actually tested?
